# Run of show (75 minutes; 60-minute cuts in brackets)

Before class: open the dashboard and the Results page (it updates live through the whole session), create the session (code e.g. ETHICS1), save default groups (the session starts in **defining**), project slide 1 with the QR code. Print the packet as backup. Check the Supabase project is awake.

Sources are not anonymized. Each card ends with its citation and link, so "who produced it" is part of what students weigh when they rate.

Slide numbers refer to `slides/output/edtech_slides_v3.pptx`.

| Clock | Phase button | Slide | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | **defining** | 1–3 | Students join by QR, pick group, submit a definition. Project the Results page to show definitions as they arrive, or read from the dashboard. |
| 0:06 | **reading** | 4–6 | Working definition and outcome. Instructions. Groups read their two cards (12 min) [8]. Ratings are open during reading. |
| 0:18 | reading | 6 | Group discussion, 8 min [6]. |
| 0:26 | **ratings** | 7 | Presentations, 2 min per group [8 min total]. Remind students to submit ratings before the lock. |
| 0:36 | **locked** | 8 | **Lock first.** Then walk through the key ideas the studies should have raised, tying each to what a group said. Show the dashboard or Results page as needed. |
| 0:50 | **standards_submit** | 9 | Up to three standards each, 4 min. Watch the count on the dashboard; delete duplicates or anything unfit for the screen. |
| 0:54 | **standards_vote** | 10 | Every submission is on the ballot, in random order per phone. Vote for up to five, 4 min. |
| 0:58 | **final** | 11 | The Standards page and dashboard show the five with the most votes, ranked. Ties are broken by earlier submission; mention it if it matters. Discuss. |
| 1:10 | closed | 12–13 | End. Export CSV if wanted. Delete the session within 30 days. |

Fallbacks: if the database fails, hand out the packet, collect paper ballots, and tally by show of hands.

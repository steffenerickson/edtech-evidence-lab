# Run of show (75 minutes; 60-minute cuts in brackets)

Before class: open the dashboard, create the session (code e.g. ETHICS1), save default groups, set phase **lobby**, project slide 1 with the QR code. Print the packet as backup. Check the Supabase project is awake.

| Clock | Phase button | Slide | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | lobby → **defining** | 1–3 | Students join by QR, pick group, submit a definition. Turn on "students can see results" to show definitions on slide 3, or read from the dashboard. |
| 0:06 | **reading** | 4–6 | Working definition and outcome. Instructions. Groups read their two cards (12 min) [8]. Ratings are open during reading. |
| 0:18 | reading | 6 | Group discussion, 8 min [6]. |
| 0:26 | **rating_pre** | 7 | Presentations, 2 min per group [8 min total]. Remind students to submit ratings before the lock. |
| 0:36 | **locked** | 8–9 | **Lock first.** Then show pre-reveal results (slide 9 or dashboard). |
| 0:41 | **revealed** | 10 | Reveal. Students open the studies page. Walk through B vs F, A vs N, G vs H, and J. |
| 0:47 | **rating_post** | 11 | Second rating for their two studies, 90 seconds. |
| 0:49 | locked view | 12–13 | Before/after per study. Who moved and why. The two cautions. |
| 0:54 | **standards_submit** | 14 | One standard each, 3 min. |
| 0:57 | **standards_sort** | 15 | Create ballot options from submissions; assign scope; tick "on ballot". 6 min. |
| 1:03 | **standards_vote** | 16 | Vote, up to five, 4 min. |
| 1:07 | **final** | 17 | Tick five winners (ties: voice vote, then tick). Discuss. |
| 1:15 | closed | 18 | End. Export CSV if wanted. Delete the session within 30 days. |

Fallbacks: if the database fails, hand out the packet, collect paper ballots, and tally by show of hands on slide 9's blank frame; reveal from slide 10's static citation list.
